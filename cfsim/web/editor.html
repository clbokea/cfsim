cfsim room editor

Turn a floor plan into a world you can fly in: python -m cfsim --world name.txt my_script.py

1Floor plan

A PNG or JPG image of the plan (export PDFs as an image first).

2Scale

Click Measure, then click two points on the plan whose distance you know (a scale bar or a dimension line), and enter it.

Not set.

3Area (optional)

Drag a rectangle around the part you want – leave out titles and dimension lines.

4Walls

Clean up: paint missing walls, erase text, furniture and door swings. Doors you want to fly through must be open (free).

5Start spots

S is where the first drone takes off (position 0, 0) and the radio beacon. Extra spots 1–9 are for swarms.

6Save

m

Open a floor plan to start.
